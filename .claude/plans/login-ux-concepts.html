Shared UX improvements

  • Remove the inactive Google sign-in button until OAuth works.
  • Keep labels visible, errors near the form, and focus rings obvious.
  • Show the theme toggle on login and registration, matching the rest of the app.
  • On mobile, use a single column with comfortable tap targets and no decorative panel.

Recommendation

Choose The Ledger. It gives the product a recognizable finance identity without adding new product behavior. Keep the current one-step email/password flow and wire the form to the existing login handler.

The sample brand names are placeholders. The dashboard’s existing blue/gray styling can transition to a restrained emerald/ink palette over time; no chart or budget features are assumed.
